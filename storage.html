Technical architecture

From stored energy to
controlled power.

A VENTRON storage system can include battery racks, battery management system, power conversion system, energy management logic, safety/protection equipment, cooling/ventilation strategy, monitoring, and integration with site loads or renewable generation.

  • Battery rack and capacity planning
  • BMS, PCS, EMS, and protection systems
  • Backup, load shifting, or hybrid operation
  • Optional solar, wind, AWG, or microgrid integration
