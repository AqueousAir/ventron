Climate-specific production

Why Every VENTRON AWG Machine Starts With Your Location.

VENTRON AWG is not a one-size-fits-all machine. Atmospheric water generation depends heavily on the customer’s site conditions, including temperature, relative humidity, airflow, daily production target, and installation environment. For this reason, each VENTRON AWG order starts with a technical review of the customer’s location and project requirements.

Humidity

Atmospheric water generation depends on temperature and humidity.

Climate

A coastal humid area is different from a dry inland or desert area.

Demand

Daily water production expectations must match real site conditions.

Configuration

Airflow, condensation load, filtration, and operating profile may change by location.
