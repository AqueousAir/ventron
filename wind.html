Technical architecture

From wind resource to
usable energy.

A wind power system can include turbine assembly, tower or support structure, generator, controller, braking/protection system, inverter interface, monitoring, and optional storage integration. Final design depends on verified site conditions and local engineering requirements.

  • Wind resource and exposure review
  • Turbine and support structure planning
  • Electrical conversion and protection
  • Optional hybrid and storage integration
