VENTRON ENERGY respects the privacy of its website visitors, customers, partners, suppliers, investors, and all individuals who interact with our digital platforms, products, services, and business operations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, disclose, and manage personal information when you visit our website, submit information through our forms, communicate with us, request information about our products, register your interest, or engage with VENTRON ENERGY in any commercial, technical, operational, or business-related capacity.

VENTRON ENERGY operates in the field of advanced energy and water technologies, including but not limited to atmospheric water generation systems, solar power solutions, wind power systems, power storage technologies, energy intelligence solutions, infrastructure products, monitoring systems, and related engineering, maintenance, deployment, and support services. Because our products and services may involve technical assessments, site evaluations, project inquiries, installation requirements, maintenance requests, monitoring services, customer support, and business communications, we may collect and process certain personal and business-related information as described in this Privacy Policy.

By accessing or using our website, submitting information through our forms, contacting us, requesting a quotation, registering interest in our products, or otherwise interacting with VENTRON ENERGY, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

01Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the VENTRON ENERGY website and through related digital communications, online forms, inquiry pages, contact forms, registration forms, newsletter subscriptions, investor or partner forms, product inquiry submissions, technical support requests, and other online or offline interactions connected to our website or commercial activities.

This Privacy Policy also applies to information collected from individuals acting on behalf of companies, organizations, government entities, contractors, suppliers, distributors, customers, or project partners. In many cases, the information we collect may relate to business representatives rather than private consumers. However, where such information identifies or can reasonably identify an individual, it is treated as personal information under this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, platforms, software, applications, payment providers, external links, social media platforms, or services that are not owned or controlled by VENTRON ENERGY. If you access third-party websites through links on our website, their own privacy policies and terms will apply.

02Information We May Collect

VENTRON ENERGY may collect different types of information depending on how you interact with us. The information we collect may include information you provide directly, information collected automatically through website technologies, and information obtained through business communications or third-party sources where permitted by law.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you contact VENTRON ENERGY, submit an inquiry, request a quotation, register interest in a product, request technical information, submit a partnership proposal, or communicate with us, we may collect information such as your name, company name, job title, email address, phone number, country, city, business address, project location, industry sector, and the nature of your inquiry.

We may also collect information relating to your interest in specific VENTRON ENERGY products or solutions, including atmospheric water generation systems, solar power systems, wind energy systems, battery storage systems, hybrid energy infrastructure, technical monitoring services, installation services, maintenance services, and any related products or services that may be introduced by VENTRON ENERGY in the future.

If you submit a technical or commercial inquiry, we may collect project-related information such as expected water production requirements, energy consumption requirements, site location, available land or rooftop space, environmental conditions, power availability, installation requirements, intended use case, operational capacity, estimated demand, project timeline, budget range, and other information necessary to evaluate your request.

2.2 Business and Commercial Information

Because VENTRON ENERGY provides technical and infrastructure-related products, we may collect business information from customers, suppliers, consultants, distributors, investors, contractors, government representatives, and project partners. This may include company registration details, business contact information, procurement requirements, project documents, technical specifications, commercial proposals, purchase requirements, delivery details, billing information, contractual correspondence, and records of communications.

Where business information contains personal information, such as the name, email address, phone number, or job title of a representative, we process that information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2.3 Technical and Website Usage Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through standard web technologies. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring website, pages visited, time spent on the website, language preferences, approximate location derived from IP address, access dates and times, interaction with website content, and other diagnostic or analytical information.

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website, improve website performance, maintain security, prevent fraud or misuse, troubleshoot technical issues, and enhance the overall user experience.

2.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, tracking technologies, and similar mechanisms to operate properly, remember user preferences, analyze website performance, understand visitor behavior, improve marketing effectiveness, and enhance website functionality.

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. Some cookies are necessary for the website to function, while others may be used for analytics, performance measurement, security, or marketing purposes. You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of our website.

2.5 Product, Service, and Support-Related Information

If you purchase, install, operate, or request support for VENTRON ENERGY products or solutions, we may collect information necessary to provide technical support, maintenance, warranty services, monitoring, diagnostics, spare parts, operational optimization, or after-sales service.

This may include product model, serial number, installation site, commissioning date, maintenance history, reported faults, service requests, operational status, performance data, system alerts, technical logs, and support communications. Where such information is connected to an identifiable individual or business representative, it will be handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Some VENTRON ENERGY products may include monitoring, control, or reporting systems. Depending on the product and service configuration, such systems may collect operational data relating to energy production, water generation, environmental conditions, system performance, battery storage status, equipment efficiency, usage patterns, and maintenance requirements. This type of data is generally technical or operational in nature, but it may be associated with a customer account, site contact, or project representative.

03How We Use Your Information

VENTRON ENERGY uses collected information for legitimate business, technical, operational, legal, and commercial purposes. These purposes may include responding to inquiries, preparing quotations, evaluating project requirements, providing product information, managing customer relationships, delivering products and services, supporting installation and maintenance, improving website performance, and complying with legal obligations.

We may use your information to communicate with you regarding your inquiry, request additional details, provide technical or commercial proposals, schedule meetings, send product information, respond to support requests, provide updates about our services, or manage ongoing business discussions.

We may also use information to assess the suitability of VENTRON ENERGY products for specific projects, including atmospheric water generation systems, solar power solutions, wind energy systems, power storage systems, and hybrid energy infrastructure. This assessment may involve reviewing climate conditions, project size, required output, expected power demand, environmental requirements, technical feasibility, and commercial considerations.

Where you are an existing customer, partner, supplier, or contractor, we may use your information to manage contracts, process orders, arrange delivery, coordinate installation, provide maintenance, issue invoices, manage warranty claims, maintain business records, and support ongoing operations.

We may use website usage information to improve the design, content, security, functionality, speed, and performance of our website. This may include analyzing which pages are visited, how users interact with content, which forms are submitted, and how website visitors arrive at our website.

We may use your information for internal business planning, research, product development, quality improvement, service optimization, market analysis, and development of future products or services. This may include improving VENTRON ENERGY’s technology offerings, customer experience, technical documentation, service models, and operational processes.

Where permitted by law, we may use your contact information to send business communications, product updates, company announcements, invitations, newsletters, marketing materials, or information about VENTRON ENERGY solutions that may be relevant to your interests. You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided in the communication or by contacting us directly.

04Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on applicable data protection laws, VENTRON ENERGY may process your personal information based on one or more legal grounds.

We may process your information where it is necessary to respond to your request, provide information you have asked for, prepare a quotation, enter into a contract, perform contractual obligations, deliver products or services, or provide support.

We may process your information where we have a legitimate business interest, provided that such interest is not overridden by your privacy rights. Legitimate interests may include operating our website, responding to business inquiries, managing customer relationships, improving our products, preventing fraud, maintaining security, conducting business analysis, and developing our services.

We may process your information where you have given consent, such as when you subscribe to receive updates or agree to certain cookies or marketing communications. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to applicable legal and contractual restrictions.

We may also process your information where required to comply with legal obligations, regulatory requirements, court orders, tax obligations, accounting rules, corporate governance requirements, or lawful requests from public authorities.

05Disclosure of Information

VENTRON ENERGY does not sell personal information. However, we may disclose information where necessary for legitimate business, operational, technical, commercial, or legal purposes.

We may share information with employees, officers, consultants, contractors, service providers, technology providers, website hosting providers, analytics providers, cloud service providers, IT support providers, logistics providers, installation partners, maintenance teams, professional advisors, legal advisors, auditors, insurers, and other parties who support our business operations.

Where necessary to respond to your inquiry or deliver a project, we may share relevant information with distributors, engineering partners, installation partners, technical consultants, manufacturers, suppliers, or project stakeholders. Such sharing will be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose.

We may disclose information in connection with a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, investment, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, joint venture, partnership, or corporate transaction involving VENTRON ENERGY. In such cases, information may be disclosed to potential investors, buyers, advisors, or transaction parties subject to appropriate confidentiality protections where applicable.

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, court order, government request, public authority request, or where disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, property, safety, customers, users, partners, employees, or the public.

06International Transfers

VENTRON ENERGY may operate, communicate, and work with service providers, partners, suppliers, customers, and contractors in different countries. As a result, personal information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, VENTRON ENERGY will take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws. These safeguards may include contractual protections, confidentiality obligations, access controls, technical security measures, and other measures designed to protect personal information.

By interacting with VENTRON ENERGY, submitting information through our website, or communicating with us, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

07Data Retention

VENTRON ENERGY retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

The period for which we retain information may depend on the nature of the information, the purpose for which it was collected, the status of our relationship with you, legal and regulatory requirements, contractual obligations, warranty periods, accounting requirements, dispute resolution needs, and legitimate business requirements.

For example, inquiry records may be retained for a reasonable period to allow follow-up communications, business development, quotation tracking, and customer relationship management. Contractual, financial, and transaction records may be retained for longer periods where required for legal, tax, accounting, audit, or regulatory purposes. Technical support records may be retained to support product performance, maintenance history, warranty management, and service continuity.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, archive, or securely restrict access to it in accordance with applicable laws and internal retention practices.

08Security of Information

VENTRON ENERGY takes reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, secure storage, password protection, data minimization, confidentiality obligations, secure communication practices, system monitoring, restricted access to sensitive information, and other safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved.

However, no website, online platform, digital transmission, email communication, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for ensuring that any information you submit to us is sent through appropriate and secure channels, especially where the information is commercially sensitive, confidential, or technical in nature.

09Operational and Technical Data

Certain VENTRON ENERGY products and services may involve the collection or processing of operational and technical data. This may include equipment performance data, energy production data, water generation output, environmental readings, battery storage performance, wind or solar generation data, fault alerts, diagnostic logs, maintenance records, and system efficiency metrics.

Such data is generally used to operate, monitor, maintain, troubleshoot, improve, and optimize VENTRON ENERGY products and services. It may also be used to identify performance issues, improve future product design, support predictive maintenance, enhance safety, reduce downtime, and provide customer support.

Where operational data is linked to a customer, site, account, or individual representative, it may be treated as personal information or confidential business information, depending on the context. VENTRON ENERGY will handle such data responsibly and use it only for legitimate technical, operational, contractual, or business purposes.

VENTRON ENERGY may also use aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified technical data for research, analytics, benchmarking, product development, performance improvement, and commercial planning. Such data will not reasonably identify an individual.

10Confidential Business Information

In the course of product inquiries, technical discussions, project evaluations, quotations, installations, partnerships, or support requests, you may provide VENTRON ENERGY with business, technical, financial, or project-related information that is confidential or commercially sensitive.

VENTRON ENERGY will take reasonable steps to protect confidential business information submitted to us. However, unless a separate written confidentiality agreement or non-disclosure agreement has been signed, information submitted through the website or general communication channels should not be considered subject to special confidentiality obligations beyond those described in this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive technical drawings, trade secrets, proprietary project documents, financial records, or confidential business information through public website forms unless specifically requested by VENTRON ENERGY or protected by an appropriate written agreement.

11Marketing Communications

VENTRON ENERGY may use your contact information to send marketing or business communications where permitted by law. These communications may include product updates, company news, technology announcements, project information, event invitations, educational content, industry insights, or updates regarding VENTRON ENERGY’s products and services.

You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option included in our emails, where available, or by contacting us directly. Even if you opt out of marketing communications, we may still send you non-marketing communications relating to active inquiries, contracts, purchases, installations, service requests, warranties, safety notices, or legal matters.

12Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These rights may include the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, the right to request deletion of your information, the right to object to certain processing activities, the right to restrict processing, the right to withdraw consent, and the right to request a copy of your information in a portable format.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations, contractual obligations, identity verification requirements, legitimate business needs, and exceptions under applicable law. For example, we may need to retain certain records for tax, accounting, legal, warranty, regulatory, or dispute resolution purposes.

To exercise any applicable rights, you may contact us using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy. We may request additional information to verify your identity before responding to your request.

The VENTRON ENERGY website and services are intended for business, commercial, technical, industrial, infrastructure, and professional purposes. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information, unless we are legally required to retain it.

You may control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow users to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive alerts when cookies are being used. However, if you disable certain cookies, parts of the website may not function properly or may not provide the intended user experience.

Where required by applicable law, we may provide a cookie banner or consent tool that allows you to manage cookie preferences. Your choices may be stored for future visits unless you clear your browser settings or use a different device or browser.

VENTRON ENERGY relies on the information you provide to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, manage communications, deliver services, and provide technical support. You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide is accurate, complete, and up to date.
